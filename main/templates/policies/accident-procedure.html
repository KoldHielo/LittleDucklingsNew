Children sometimes have accidents, even in a carefully planned setting. Their safety, comfort and immediate care will always come first.

Immediate response

  • I will make the area safe, assess the child and provide only first aid for which I am trained.
  • I will call 999 for a life-threatening emergency or serious injury, or NHS 111 when urgent clinical advice is appropriate. I will not delay treatment while trying to contact a parent.
  • I will keep other children safely supervised, follow any individual healthcare plan, and accompany the child to hospital where necessary until a parent or authorised adult takes over.
  • I will contact the parent or carer promptly.

Keeping families informed

I record every accident or injury and any first aid given in ChildLogs; I do not keep separate paper accident forms. The record includes what happened, when and where it happened, the injury, treatment, who was present and any follow-up. Parents can see and acknowledge the record through the ChildLogs parent portal. I will tell them on the same day, or as soon as reasonably possible. Any important injury reported or noticed when a child arrives will also be added to ChildLogs.

When I must tell other organisations

  • I will tell Ofsted about a serious accident, injury, illness or death while a child is in my care, including what I did. I will do this as soon as reasonably possible and always within 14 days using the official serious childcare incident service.
  • I will contact Stoke-on-Trent Integrated Front Door and follow its advice if the incident raises safeguarding concerns or involves serious injury or death. I will call 999 if anyone is in immediate danger.
  • I will report food poisoning affecting two or more children to Ofsted within the same 14-day limit and contact the local Health Protection Team where UKHSA guidance requires it.
  • I will make a RIDDOR report only when the event meets the criteria in the Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations 2013, taking advice from the Health and Safety Executive where needed.
  • I will notify my insurer and any other relevant body where the policy, contract or law requires it.

Afterwards

I will replace any first-aid supplies used and look at whether anything needs to change to prevent the same thing happening again. Accident records are kept securely in ChildLogs for the period explained in the UK GDPR and Data Protection/Retention Policy.
