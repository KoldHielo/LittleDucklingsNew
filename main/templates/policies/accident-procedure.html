This procedure explains how I prevent, respond to, record and report accidents, injuries, serious illness and deaths. The child's safety and immediate care always come first.

Immediate response

  • I will make the area safe, assess the child and provide only first aid for which I am trained.
  • I will call 999 for a life-threatening emergency or serious injury, or NHS 111 when urgent clinical advice is appropriate. I will not delay treatment while trying to contact a parent.
  • I will keep other children safely supervised, follow any individual healthcare plan, and accompany the child to hospital where necessary until a parent or authorised adult takes over.
  • I will contact the parent or carer promptly.

Recording and informing parents

I will make a written record of every accident or injury and any first aid given. It will include the child, date, time, place, circumstances, injury, treatment, people present, and action or follow-up. Parents will be informed on the same day, or as soon as reasonably practicable, and asked to acknowledge the record. I will also record significant pre-existing injuries reported or seen on arrival.

External notifications

  • I will notify Ofsted of a serious accident, serious injury, serious illness or death while a child is in my care, and the action taken, as soon as reasonably practicable and always within 14 days using the official serious childcare incident service.
  • I will notify Stoke-on-Trent Children's Social Care and follow its advice where the incident raises safeguarding concerns or involves serious injury or death. Immediate danger is reported to 999.
  • I will report food poisoning affecting two or more children to Ofsted within the same 14-day limit and contact the local Health Protection Team where UKHSA guidance requires it.
  • I will make a RIDDOR report only when the event meets the criteria in the Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations 2013, taking advice from the Health and Safety Executive where needed.
  • I will notify my insurer and any other relevant body where the policy, contract or law requires it.

Review and retention

I will replenish first-aid supplies, review the risk assessment and supervision arrangements, and record actions taken. Records are stored securely and retained according to the UK GDPR and Data Protection/Retention Policy. Any long retention period is based on documented insurance and limitation-risk advice, not described as an EYFS or UK GDPR requirement.
