I use ChildLogs to keep an accurate daily register showing when each child arrives and leaves, including times when I take them to or collect them from another setting. Parents can view their child's information through the ChildLogs parent portal.

What families must do

Please let me know as soon as possible if your child will be away, why they will be absent and roughly how long for. Please keep me updated and make sure I have current contact details and, where possible, more than two emergency contact numbers.

Unexpected or prolonged absence

  • If an expected child does not arrive and I have not heard from you, I will contact the parents or carers straight away and then try the emergency contacts.
  • I will record the absence, explanation and contact attempts in ChildLogs. I will also be mindful of repeated absences or explanations that cause concern.
  • If I cannot confirm that the child is safe, or I am worried by the explanation, I will follow my Safeguarding Policy. This may mean contacting Integrated Front Door straight away or asking the police to make a welfare check.
  • Where funded early education applies, I will also follow the local authority funding agreement and share attendance information lawfully.

Information use and retention

Attendance records are kept securely in ChildLogs. When needed, I may share them with Ofsted, Stoke-on-Trent City Council, Integrated Front Door, HMRC, insurers or the police. They are kept only for the time explained in my retention schedule.
