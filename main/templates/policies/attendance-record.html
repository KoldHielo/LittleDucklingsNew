I keep an accurate daily record of each child's name and hours of attendance, including arrival, departure, and transfers to or from another setting. This supports safety, safeguarding, funding and the current EYFS.

What families must do

Parents and carers must tell me as soon as possible if a child will be absent, explain why, give the likely duration, and keep me updated. They must provide current contact details and, where possible, more than two emergency contact numbers.

Unexpected or prolonged absence

  • If an expected child does not arrive and I have not been told why, I will contact the parents or carers without delay and then the emergency contacts.
  • I will record the absence, reasons given, contact attempts and response; notice patterns or unusual explanations; and consider the child's vulnerabilities, family circumstances and any existing plans.
  • If I cannot establish that the child is safe, or the explanation creates concern, I will follow the safeguarding policy. This may include immediate referral to Children's Social Care or requesting a police welfare check. I will not wait for a pattern where there is an immediate concern.
  • Where funded early education applies, I will also follow the local authority funding agreement and share attendance information lawfully.

Information use and retention

Attendance records may be shared where necessary with Ofsted, Stoke-on-Trent City Council, safeguarding partners, HMRC, insurers or law-enforcement bodies. They are access-controlled and kept only for the period in the retention schedule; UK GDPR is not itself a reason to keep them for 21 years and 3 months.
