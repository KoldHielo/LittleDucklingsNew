I support behaviour through secure relationships, clear and consistent boundaries, and developmentally appropriate co-regulation. Behaviour is communication: I look for unmet needs, feelings, communication differences and environmental triggers.

Positive support

  • I model kindness, explain simple expectations, notice positive choices, name feelings, offer limited choices, redirect or distract, and help children repair relationships.
  • I work with parents so approaches are consistent and take account of age, development, culture, trauma, communication and SEND. Reasonable adjustments and individual support plans are used where needed.
  • I do not use humiliation, isolation as punishment, food deprivation, withholding comfort, shouting, labels, punitive time-out or any punishment that could negatively affect a child's wellbeing.
  • Corporal punishment and threats of corporal punishment are never used or permitted.

Physical intervention

Physical intervention is used only when absolutely necessary to avert immediate danger of personal injury or serious damage to property, and is reasonable, proportionate and for the shortest possible time. It is never used to force compliance or as punishment.

Every physical intervention is recorded with the circumstances, method, duration, people present, injuries and follow-up. The parent or carer is informed on the same day or as soon as reasonably practicable. I review triggers, the risk assessment and the child's plan, and seek professional advice where appropriate. Any safeguarding concern is managed under the Safeguarding Policy.
