I help children learn about feelings, kindness and safe behaviour through warm relationships and clear, consistent boundaries. Behaviour is often a child's way of communicating, so I look at what they may be feeling or needing.

Positive support

  • I model kindness, explain simple expectations, notice positive choices, name feelings, offer limited choices, redirect or distract, and help children repair relationships.
  • I work with parents so approaches are consistent and take account of age, development, culture, trauma, communication and SEND. Reasonable adjustments and individual support plans are used where needed.

Physical intervention

I will physically intervene only when I must act straight away to stop someone being hurt or serious damage being caused. I will use the least intervention needed for the shortest possible time. It will never be used as punishment or simply to make a child obey.

I will record what happened in ChildLogs and tell the parent or carer on the same day, or as soon as reasonably possible. Afterwards, I will look at what led to the situation and whether the child's support needs to change.
