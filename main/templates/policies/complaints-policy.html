I welcome concerns and aim to resolve them fairly, promptly and without disadvantaging a child or family. A concern can be raised in person, by telephone, or by email to info@littleducklingschildminding.co.uk. A complaint about fulfilment of EYFS requirements should be made in writing so the formal process can begin.

How I handle a written complaint

  • I will acknowledge it, clarify the issues, assess immediate safety and conflicts of interest, and investigate proportionately.
  • I will keep a record of the complaint, evidence considered, findings, action taken and outcome.
  • I will notify the complainant of the outcome within 28 days of receiving the written complaint.
  • I will keep complaint records securely and make the relevant records and outcome available to Ofsted on request. A non-identifying summary will be available to parents on request.

Safeguarding and regulatory concerns

A complaint does not replace safeguarding action. A concern about a child's safety is referred immediately under the Safeguarding Policy. An allegation about an adult who lives, works or looks after children at the premises is referred to the LADO/Children's Social Care and notified to Ofsted as applicable; I will not investigate it first in a way that could compromise those processes.

Taking a concern further

Little Ducklings is registered directly with Ofsted. Ofsted may consider regulatory concerns separately from my complaints process but cannot resolve private contractual disputes. Contact Ofsted at enquiries@ofsted.gov.uk or 0300 123 4666, or use the official childcare complaints guidance. If a child is at risk, call Stoke-on-Trent Integrated Front Door on 01782 235100, the out-of-hours Emergency Duty Team on 01782 234234, or 999 in an emergency.

Complaint records are retained for the period in the published retention schedule and reviewed against applicable register and insurer requirements.
