Data controller: Laura Oldfield, trading as Little Ducklings Childminding. Contact: info@littleducklingschildminding.co.uk or 07547 635016.

A video doorbell monitors the entrance and the area immediately in front of it to help control access, prevent crime, protect people and property, and ensure children are released only to authorised adults. Clear signage is placed before visitors enter the camera's field of view.

How recording is used

  • The lawful basis is legitimate interests in safeguarding and premises security. I have assessed necessity, proportionality and the effect on visitors and neighbours, and review that assessment annually.
  • The field of view is kept to the minimum necessary and adjusted to avoid neighbouring homes and unnecessary public-space capture. Facial recognition is not used.
  • The system is configured for video; audio recording is not used. Any future change would require a fresh assessment and updated notice before activation.
  • Routine footage is automatically overwritten after no more than 30 days. A relevant clip may be isolated for longer while an incident, complaint, insurance claim or legal process is active, then deleted when no longer required.

Storage, access and sharing

Recordings are held in the doorbell supplier's encrypted cloud service. Access is limited to Laura and one authorised household member, each using an individual secured account and bound by confidentiality. Access is reviewed and recorded where the system permits. Footage is disclosed only where necessary and lawful, for example to police, Children's Social Care, Ofsted, insurers, legal advisers, or an individual exercising a data right. It is never posted publicly or used for marketing.

The supplier may process data outside the UK. Its current location and transfer safeguards are checked in the processor register; where required, an adequacy regulation or approved UK transfer mechanism is used. Supplier details and safeguards are available on request.

Your rights

You may ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or object to processing, although another person's privacy, safeguarding duties or legal obligations can limit what can be supplied or deleted. Contact me promptly with the date and approximate time so footage is not overwritten. You may complain to me first and then to the Information Commissioner's Office.
