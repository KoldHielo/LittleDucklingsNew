Who is responsible for the recordings: Laura Oldfield, trading as Little Ducklings Childminding. You can contact me at info@littleducklingschildminding.co.uk or 07547 635016.

I have a doorbell camera at the entrance to my home. It helps me see who is at the door, keep the setting secure and make sure children leave only with an authorised adult. It records both video and audio when it is triggered.

There is not currently a separate CCTV sign outside. The doorbell itself is visible, parents are told about it when their child joins, and this notice explains how it is used.

Why I use it

  • I use the recordings for the legitimate interests of keeping children, visitors and the property safe.
  • The camera is aimed at the entrance and captures only the area needed for this purpose as far as the equipment allows. It does not use facial recognition.
  • Because audio is more private than images alone, I will listen to it only when it is relevant to a security, collection, safeguarding or incident concern.

Where recordings are kept

The recordings are stored locally on a micro SD card inside the doorbell. They are not stored in a cloud service. Older recordings are overwritten on a rolling basis when the card becomes full. If a clip is needed for an incident, safeguarding concern, complaint or insurance matter, I may keep a separate copy until the matter is finished and then delete it safely.

Access is limited to me and one trusted household member. Recordings are never posted online or used for advertising. I share a recording only when there is a clear and lawful reason, for example with the police, Integrated Front Door, Ofsted, an insurer or legal adviser.

If you have a question about a recording

You can ask to see footage of yourself, ask me to delete it or object to its use. Sometimes I may need to protect another person's privacy or keep a recording for safeguarding or legal reasons. Please contact me quickly with the date and approximate time, as older footage may already have been overwritten. If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office.
