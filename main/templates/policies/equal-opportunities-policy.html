I provide an inclusive, anti-discriminatory setting in which every child and family is treated with dignity, concern and respect. I challenge racism, prejudice, harassment, victimisation and discriminatory language or behaviour.

Equality Act commitments

I do not unlawfully discriminate because of age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, pregnancy and maternity, race, religion or belief, sex, or sexual orientation. I also value differences in family structure, socio-economic background, care experience, health, home language and immigration status without incorrectly presenting these as additional Equality Act protected characteristics.

  • I make reasonable adjustments for disabled children and anticipate barriers to access, participation, communication and learning.
  • I plan support with the child and family and, with an appropriate lawful basis, relevant professionals. Fees, admissions and routines are applied fairly.
  • Books, play, displays and conversation reflect varied families, cultures, abilities and identities without stereotyping. Home languages and cultural practices are respected.
  • I use culturally informed, anti-racist and anti-discriminatory safeguarding practice and remain alert to bias affecting how concerns are recognised or responded to.
  • Incidents are recorded, addressed developmentally, shared with families when appropriate and used to review provision.

Concerns can be raised under the Complaints Policy. SEND support and the graduated approach are explained in the SEND Policy.
