I process personal data in accordance with the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, as amended. I keep a data inventory, processor register and retention log, and apply lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, security and accountability.

Information and lawful use

Records may include identity and contact details; contracts, fees and attendance; health, allergy, dietary, SEND and care information; learning records and images; accidents, medication and complaints; safeguarding information; website enquiries; and video-doorbell footage. Depending on the purpose, the lawful basis is contract, legal obligation, vital interests, legitimate interests, public task where applicable, or consent. Health, SEND and other special-category data also requires an Article 9 condition, commonly health/social care, substantial public interest safeguarding, legal claims, or explicit consent where genuinely optional.

Information may be shared without consent where another lawful basis permits or requires it, including safeguarding, emergency care, regulation, funding, tax, insurance or legal claims. Only necessary information is shared.

Security, systems and processors

Paper records are locked; electronic records are protected by device security, strong unique passwords, access controls, updates and appropriate backup. Current systems include the public website contact form and hosting, business email, local secured devices/backups, and the video-doorbell cloud service. There is no parent login or online child-record portal. Processor contracts, locations and international-transfer safeguards are recorded and reviewed. Access is limited to authorised people with confidentiality duties.

Periods are reviewed against current insurer terms, funding agreements and law. A legal hold pauses deletion. At expiry, paper is cross-cut shredded and electronic copies securely deleted, including recoverable copies where practicable.

Rights, breaches and review

People may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, or object, and may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Rights are not absolute where law, safeguarding, another person's rights or legal claims require retention or restricted disclosure. Requests and data complaints should be sent to info@littleducklingschildminding.co.uk.

I document and contain personal-data breaches, assess risk, and notify the ICO without undue delay and, where required, within 72 hours; affected people are told where high risk requires it. Data and retention are reviewed at least annually and when systems or practice change. See the Privacy Notice for full transparency information.
