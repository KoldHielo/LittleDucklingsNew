I take families' privacy seriously. I follow the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 and collect only the information I need to provide safe, well-organised childcare.

What I keep and why

Childcare records can include names and contact details, contracts, fees, attendance, health and allergy information, SEND and care needs, learning updates and photographs, accidents, medicine, complaints and safeguarding information. I use this information to provide childcare, keep children safe and meet my legal, Ofsted, funding, tax and insurance duties.

The legal reason will depend on what the information is used for. It may be needed for our contract, a legal duty, someone's vital interests, a task linked to funded childcare, or my legitimate interests in running a safe service. I use consent where families have a real choice, such as some uses of photographs. Extra protection applies to health, SEND and safeguarding information.

Sometimes I can or must share information without asking for permission first—for example, to protect a child, arrange emergency care, meet an Ofsted or funding duty, deal with tax or insurance, or respond to a legal claim. I share only what is needed.

ChildLogs and security

All childcare records are stored electronically in ChildLogs; I do not keep a separate set of paper records. ChildLogs provides the childcare system and parent portal and acts as my data processor. It may hold child and family details, attendance, daily care, sleep, food, medication, accidents and incidents, observations, photographs, documents, signatures, visitor and invoice information, depending on the features I use.

Parents receive their own ChildLogs portal access so they can see the records made available for their child. Accounts and devices must be protected with strong passwords and used only by authorised people. ChildLogs uses access controls, encrypted connections and suitable backup and security measures. Its own privacy policy and data-processing terms explain its systems and subprocessors.

The public Little Ducklings website remains a showcase site and has no login. Website enquiries arrive through business email. Doorbell recordings are held separately on the micro SD card inside the doorbell, not in ChildLogs or cloud storage.

I review these times against current law, funding rules and insurer advice. If a complaint, safeguarding matter or legal claim is still open, the relevant record may need to be kept longer. When a record reaches the end of its period, I delete it securely from ChildLogs or ask ChildLogs to help where needed. Protected backup copies may remain until the normal backup cycle overwrites them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see your information, correct it, limit its use, object, ask for it in a portable form where this applies, or ask for deletion. You can also change your mind where I rely on consent. Some information must still be kept for safeguarding, legal or insurance reasons, or to protect another person's privacy. Please email info@littleducklingschildminding.co.uk.

If information is lost, shared with the wrong person or accessed improperly, I will act quickly to contain it and understand the risk. I will notify the ICO within 72 hours where the law requires this and tell affected families where the risk is high. See my Privacy Notice for more information.
