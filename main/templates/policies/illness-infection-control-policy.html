I do all I reasonably can to stop infections spreading while still making sure an unwell child feels cared for. This includes good handwashing, ventilation, cleaning, safe food handling and careful cleaning of bodily fluids. Please tell me promptly about symptoms or a diagnosis that may affect other children.

Attendance and exclusion

I follow the live UKHSA public-health exclusion table rather than a fixed list that may become outdated. Children with diarrhoea or vomiting stay away until 48 hours after the last episode unless UKHSA/clinical advice specifies otherwise. There is no blanket 10-day COVID-19 exclusion: a person with more than mild symptoms, such as a high temperature, should stay away in line with current UKHSA guidance.

A child should also remain at home when too unwell to take part in normal routines or when their needs cannot be met safely alongside other children. Decisions take account of symptoms, clinical advice and children at higher risk.

If a child becomes ill

  • I will comfort and supervise the child, separate them from others only where appropriate without leaving them unsupervised, and follow their healthcare plan.
  • I will contact the parent for prompt collection, call NHS 111 for urgent advice or 999 in an emergency, and administer medicine only under the Medication Policy.
  • I will clean affected areas safely, record relevant care in ChildLogs and tell other families about possible exposure where advised, without naming the child unnecessarily.

Advice and notification

I will contact the local UKHSA Health Protection Team in the circumstances set out by UKHSA, including suspected outbreaks or particular infections, and follow its control advice. I will notify Ofsted of serious illness and food poisoning affecting two or more children as soon as reasonably practicable and always within 14 days, including the action taken. Other serious events are reported under the Accident Procedure.

Health information is shared only where necessary and lawful and handled under the Privacy Notice and retention policy.
