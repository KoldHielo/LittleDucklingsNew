I prevent children becoming lost through active supervision, regular headcounts, clear boundaries, risk-assessed outings and appropriate use of pushchairs, harnesses or hand-holding based on age and need.

Immediate action

  • I will immediately account for all children and keep the other children safely supervised. They will not be left while I search.
  • I will alert venue staff/security, give a description and follow the venue's lost-child procedure, including securing exits and checking CCTV where available.
  • I will call police promptly on 999; venue searches will not delay emergency contact. I will follow police instructions and then notify the child's parents or carers.
  • I will preserve relevant information including the last known location, time, clothing, circumstances and people present.

After the incident

I will record a full timeline and action taken, support the child and other children, notify parents, inform my insurer and local agencies as appropriate, and consider whether Ofsted notification is required as a significant event. I will review supervision and risk assessments, document lessons and implement changes before repeating the activity.

A child who has not been collected is managed under the separate Uncollected Child Policy.
