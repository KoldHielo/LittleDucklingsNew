I will give medicine only when a child needs it and I can do so safely. I must have written permission for that particular medicine before giving either prescription or non-prescription medicine. Permission can be given through ChildLogs, by text or by email, but verbal permission on its own is not enough.

Before administration

  • Prescription medicine must have been prescribed for that child.
  • Parents provide the original labelled container with the child's name, medicine, strength, dose, route, frequency, expiry, storage instructions and reason. I check that instructions and consent agree and clarify any discrepancy before giving it.
  • Long-term or emergency medicine is supported by an individual healthcare/allergy plan and appropriate training. I may decline administration I cannot perform safely and will agree an alternative plan with the family and clinician.

Storage and administration

Medicine is inaccessible to children, in original packaging and stored according to its instructions; refrigeration is used only where required, in a secure labelled container. Emergency medicine remains readily accessible to the responsible adult. Expiry dates are checked and unused or expired medicine returned to the parent for safe disposal.

Before giving anything, I check the right child, medicine, dose, method and time. I record it straight away in ChildLogs, including the reason and any refusal or reaction. Parents can see and acknowledge the entry through their parent portal, and I will tell them on the same day or as soon as reasonably possible.

Errors, refusal or reaction

I will never force a child to take medicine. If a dose is refused, given incorrectly or causes a reaction, I will follow the child's plan and medicine instructions, contact their parent and call NHS 111 or 999 when needed. I will record what happened in ChildLogs, tell the family openly and review how to prevent it happening again.
