Most enrolment, emergency and statutory information is required to provide safe, lawful childcare or enter the contract. Without it I may be unable to offer care. Optional processing is clearly identified. Information normally comes from parents/carers and the child, and may also come from health, education, local-authority or safeguarding professionals.

Who receives information

Only necessary information is shared with appropriate recipients: parents and authorised carers; schools/settings; health and emergency services; Stoke-on-Trent City Council and safeguarding partners; LADO; police; Ofsted; HMRC; insurers and professional advisers. Processors may include website hosting/contact-form delivery, business email, secured device/backup services and the video-doorbell cloud supplier. Processor details are maintained in my data inventory and are available on request.

Storage, transfers and retention

Records may be paper or electronic. Paper is locked; devices and accounts use access controls and security safeguards. The showcase website has no login or child-record portal. Some technology suppliers may process data outside the UK; I check the location and use an adequacy regulation or approved UK transfer safeguard where required. The record-by-record periods and secure deletion process are in the UK GDPR and Data Protection/Retention Policy.

Your rights

Depending on the legal basis, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or portability, and withdraw consent. These rights are not absolute; safeguarding, another person's privacy, regulatory duties and legal claims may restrict disclosure or deletion. There is no solely automated decision-making or profiling with legal or similarly significant effects.

Send a request or data-protection complaint to the controller details above. I may verify identity and will respond within the applicable legal period. If you remain unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office; childcare or safeguarding concerns may separately be taken to Ofsted or Children's Social Care.
