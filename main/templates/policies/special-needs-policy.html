I am responsible for the SEND/SENCO function in this childminding setting and maintain arrangements to support children with special educational needs and disabilities in line with EYFS 3.76, the Equality Act 2010 and, where locally funded places are delivered, the SEND Code of Practice and funding agreement.

Identifying and meeting need

  • I learn from the child and family about strengths, interests, routines, communication, health and barriers, and use observation and professional judgement alongside the EYFS. Developmental guidance is not used as a pass/fail checklist.
  • I discuss concerns sensitively and early with parents and listen to their views. A diagnosis is not required before support or reasonable adjustments begin.
  • I use the graduated approach: assess need and barriers; plan outcomes, adjustments, support and a review date with the family; do the agreed support; and review its effect with the child and family.
  • Activities, communication, resources, routines and the environment are adapted so the child can participate. Individual healthcare, allergy and safeguarding needs are integrated without lowering expectations or dignity.
  • With consent where that is the appropriate basis, I work with health visitors, therapists, early-years SEND services, other settings and the local authority. Information may be shared without consent where safeguarding or another legal basis requires it.

Partnership and transitions

Plans have clear outcomes, responsibilities and review dates. I support transitions to another setting or school through visits, consistent strategies and timely information sharing. Where needs may require provision beyond ordinarily available support, I help families access advice, inclusion funding or an EHC needs assessment route.

Local help and the Stoke-on-Trent graduated approach are available through the SEND Local Offer. Concerns should first be discussed with me and may then be raised under the Complaints Policy; families can also use the Local Offer's advice and feedback routes.
