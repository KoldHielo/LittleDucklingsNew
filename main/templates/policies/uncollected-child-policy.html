Parents must collect at the agreed time or notify me as early as possible of a delay and agree a safe alternative. Children are released only to a parent or an adult authorised by the parent whose identity I can verify. I will not release a child to an adult who appears unsafe through intoxication, impairment or threatening behaviour; I will follow safeguarding procedures.

If nobody arrives

  • I will continue to supervise, reassure and meet the child's needs. The child will not be left alone or transported home without prior agreement.
  • I will repeatedly try every parent/carer number and then authorised emergency contacts, recording the time and outcome of each attempt.
  • If contact cannot be made within a reasonable period, or earlier where circumstances create concern, I will seek advice from Stoke-on-Trent Integrated Front Door on 01782 235100 or the Emergency Duty Team on 01782 234234 out of hours. I will contact police on 999 for immediate danger or 101 for a welfare concern as advised.
  • I will follow agency instructions. I will not release the child to an unauthorised person or leave them at an address without a responsible authorised adult.

Follow-up

I will record the circumstances, contact attempts, decisions, collection and any agency advice; discuss the incident with the parent when safe; consider patterns and vulnerabilities; and make a safeguarding referral where indicated. I will review emergency contacts and collection arrangements. Repeated late collection may lead to contract review and an agreed late fee only where the childcare contract provides for it. A significant event will be notified to Ofsted where required.
