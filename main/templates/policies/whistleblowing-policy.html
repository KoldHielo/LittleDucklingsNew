Everyone should feel able to speak up if they believe children are being put at risk or something is being hidden. This policy is for assistants, volunteers, students, household members, parents and other professionals. Anyone raising an honest concern will be treated with respect and will not be punished for speaking up.

What and how to report

Please speak up about abuse or neglect, unsafe care, something being covered up, legal requirements not being followed, possible crime, or any risk to a child's welfare. Share the facts, dates or witnesses you already know about, but do not investigate or delay while trying to find proof.

  • Where appropriate, report promptly to Laura Oldfield in person or in writing. The concern will be acknowledged and recorded.
  • If the concern is about Laura, cannot safely be raised internally, or has not been addressed, contact the appropriate external body directly.
  • Immediate danger: police 999. Non-emergency crime: 101. Concern about a child's safety: Integrated Front Door 01782 235100, or Emergency Duty Team 01782 234234 out of hours.
  • Allegation about an adult working/living at the setting: LADO through Integrated Front Door 01782 235100 and Ofsted as applicable.
  • Ofsted: 0300 123 4666 or enquiries@ofsted.gov.uk.
  • NSPCC Whistleblowing Advice Line: 0800 028 0285 or help@nspcc.org.uk.

What happens after a report

I will first make sure children are safe, keep any evidence secure and contact the right organisation. I will not carry out my own questioning if that could affect an official investigation. I will keep a secure ChildLogs record of the concern, decisions, referrals, action and outcome.

I will give the person who raised the concern as much feedback as I legally can, although privacy may limit the detail. I will protect their identity as far as possible, but may have to share it to protect a child or ensure a fair process. Nobody will be treated badly for raising an honest concern. If the concern is not handled properly, it should be raised directly with one of the organisations above.

A concern about a child goes immediately through the Safeguarding Policy; this whistleblowing process must never delay protection.
