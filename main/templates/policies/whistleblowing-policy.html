This procedure enables an assistant, volunteer, student, household member, parent or professional to report unsafe, unlawful or improper practice affecting children. The welfare of children comes first. A person raising a genuine concern will not be victimised or disadvantaged, even if the concern is not substantiated.

What and how to report

Raise concerns about abuse or neglect, unsafe care, concealment, failure to follow statutory requirements, criminal conduct or risk to children's rights or welfare. Give factual detail, dates, records and witnesses if already known, but do not investigate or delay a report to collect proof.

  • Where appropriate, report promptly to Laura Oldfield in person or in writing. The concern will be acknowledged and recorded.
  • If the concern is about Laura, cannot safely be raised internally, or has not been addressed, contact the appropriate external body directly.
  • Immediate danger: police 999. Non-emergency crime: 101. Concern about a child's safety: Integrated Front Door 01782 235100, or Emergency Duty Team 01782 234234 out of hours.
  • Allegation about an adult working/living at the setting: LADO through Integrated Front Door 01782 235100 and Ofsted as applicable.
  • Ofsted: 0300 123 4666 or enquiries@ofsted.gov.uk.
  • NSPCC Whistleblowing Advice Line: 0800 028 0285 or help@nspcc.org.uk.

What happens after a report

I will immediately assess safety and take proportionate protective action; preserve evidence; refer safeguarding, allegation, regulatory or criminal matters to the proper body; and avoid an internal investigation that could compromise its work. I will keep a secure record of the concern, decisions, referrals, action and outcome.

The reporter will receive appropriate feedback where lawful, recognising that confidentiality and another person's rights may limit detail. Their identity is protected as far as possible but cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is required for child protection, fairness or law. Retaliation is not tolerated and will itself be addressed. If the response is inadequate, the reporter should escalate directly using the contacts above.

A concern about a child goes immediately through the Safeguarding Policy; this whistleblowing process must never delay protection.
